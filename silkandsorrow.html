Short Description

A hand-drawn, dark fantasy co-op adventure. Two orphans stumble into a hidden world where all the unwanted from humanity ends up. Together, they must explore a haunting landscape, survive its twisted horrors, and bring back light to the Chrysalis.

About the Game

Hidden below lies the Chrysalis, a forgotten landscape made from what others discarded: trash, ignored memories, emotions, and even people. Its glow once gave them new life, but now the Chrysalis is shrouded in darkness.

Silk & Sorrow is a tense test of teamwork designed exclusively for two players combining their wits to survive, playable in couch co-op and online co-op.

Key Features

  • Made for two: designed exclusively for two players, with couch co-op and online co-op.
  • Watch each other's back: trust one another to survive the strange creatures of the Chrysalis.
  • Think together: communicate and combine your wits to solve various enviromental puzzles.
  • Bring back light to the Chrysalis: explore strange ecosystems and uncover the source of the spreading corruption before everything is lost.
  • Adopt Glint the Glowgrub: a small companion whose glow lights the way. Feed it fireflies to make it stronger and unlock powerful abilities.
  • Explore a hand-drawn world: journey through landscapes made of trash, ignored memories, emotions and even people.
